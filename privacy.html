Privacy Policy

Last Updated: February 2026

Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how Truxim collects, uses, and protects information you provide when using truxim.com or purchasing our products. By accessing our website or purchasing products, you agree to the terms of this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with our practices, please do not use our website or purchase our products.

Information We Collect

We collect information in several ways:

  • Email address: Collected when you subscribe to our email list or download free resources such as our free checklist
  • Purchase information: Processed through third-party platforms including Etsy, Amazon, and Gumroad. These platforms collect and process payment and delivery information according to their own privacy policies
  • Website usage data: Collected through analytics tools and cookies to understand how visitors interact with our site
  • Contact form information: Any information you voluntarily provide through contact forms on our website

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

  • To deliver purchased products to you
  • To send requested resources such as free checklists and guides
  • To communicate product updates with your consent
  • To improve our website, products, and customer experience
  • To comply with legal obligations and protect our rights

Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services to operate our business:

  • Email marketing: Brevo (formerly Sendinblue) to send email communications
  • Sales processing: Etsy, Amazon, and Gumroad process and handle all purchase transactions. Each of these platforms maintains their own privacy policies governing how they handle your information
  • Website analytics: Tools to help us understand website performance and user behavior

We do not sell your personal information to third parties. However, when you make a purchase, your information is shared with the payment platform you choose (Etsy, Amazon, or Gumroad) according to their privacy policies.

Email Subscriptions

Email subscriptions are processed using Brevo. Information submitted through embedded forms is stored and processed in accordance with Brevo's privacy policy.

Data Storage and Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, alteration, and destruction. However, no method of electronic storage is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security of your information.

We retain your information only as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this policy or as required by law.

Cookies

Our website may use cookies for analytics, functionality, and user experience purposes. Cookies are small files stored on your device that help us recognize you and understand your preferences. You can control cookie settings through your browser preferences. Please note that disabling cookies may affect certain website features.

Your Rights

You have the following rights regarding your personal information:

  • You may request access to your personal information
  • You may request correction of inaccurate information
  • You may request deletion of your personal information
  • You may unsubscribe from email communications at any time using the unsubscribe link in our emails

To exercise these rights or request information about your data, please contact us at info@truxim.com.

Children's Privacy

Our products and services are intended for adults age 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 18. If we become aware that we have collected information from a child under 18, we will delete such information promptly.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in our practices, technology, or applicable laws. When we make material changes, we will update the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this policy regularly to stay informed about how we protect your information.

Contact

If you have questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or our privacy practices, please contact us at info@truxim.com.
