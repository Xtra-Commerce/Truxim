Divorce Document Starter Checklist

US Edition

Before meeting with a divorce attorney, one of the most valuable things you can do is begin gathering fundamental documents. The right records establish the foundation for productive professional conversations.

Without organized records, professional meetings become less productive and important details risk being overlooked.

This checklist follows Phase 1 of the Truxim Method: Document. It covers essential document categories including personal identification, financial accounts, tax returns, real estate, insurance, and more.

It is designed as a starting point to help you identify what you have and what you still need to locate.
