Truxim Divorce Prep System

US Edition | Version 1.0

A structured document organization and preparation workbook built on the Truxim Method.

Built on the Truxim Method

This system applies the four-phase Truxim Method in a structured way, specifically for divorce preparation in the United States. Each section maps to a phase, so you always know where you are and what comes next.

Learn more about the Truxim Method

What It Includes

Relationship and Jurisdiction Overview

Key dates, marriage information, and jurisdiction details that shape your preparation.

Financial Documentation and Asset Inventory

Structured documentation across assets, liabilities, income, property, and equity compensation.

Liability and Expense Organization

Clear tracking of debts, recurring costs, monthly expenses, and spending patterns.

Professional Meeting Preparation

Discussion points, communication logs, and organized materials for meetings with attorneys and advisors.

Transition and Administrative Reset

Account updates, insurance transitions, beneficiary reviews, and post-divorce planning.

Who It Is For

  • People preparing for or currently navigating a divorce in the United States
  • People who want to be organized and prepared before meeting with an attorney
  • People who need to compile financial records for disclosure and settlement discussions
  • People managing complex assets including retirement accounts, stock options, business interests, or significant property holdings
  • People going through a transition who want a clear system rather than scattered notes and documents

Who It Is Not For

  • It is not legal advice. Consult a licensed attorney for legal matters.
  • It is not financial planning or financial advice. Work with a CPA or financial advisor for your specific situation.
  • It is not a substitute for an attorney, CPA, or financial advisor. Use it alongside professional support.
  • It is not designed for jurisdictions outside the United States.

Why It Is Different

A preparation system, not a template collection. Guides you through the full scope of divorce preparation in a structured sequence.

Follows the Truxim Method. Four phases shape how information flows: Document, Organize, Clarify, and Transition.

Covers what is often missed. Goes deeper than standard checklists with equity compensation, communication logging, and post-divorce administrative planning.

Designed for use alongside professionals. Helps you work more effectively with your attorney, accountant, and financial advisor. Does not replace professional guidance.
